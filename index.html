Party

A personal iOS app for tracking bikes, mileage, and component maintenance.

Party is a non-commercial app for personal use. It stores data about bikes, installed components, their wear, and service history. Optionally, the app can sync with Strava to automatically pull in ride distance.

Privacy Policy

When Strava is connected, the app only requests access to the user's list of bikes (gear) and activities, in order to match rides with locally stored bikes and add their distance to the mileage total. Strava access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the device in the Keychain and are never sent to any third-party server — the app has no backend of its own. All other data (bikes, components, service history) is stored locally on the user's device only. Data is not sold or shared with third parties.

The user can disconnect Strava at any time in the app's settings; this does not delete already-saved local data.
