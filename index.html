Party — Strava Integration

How Party uses your Strava data

Party is an iOS app for tracking bicycles, components, and maintenance history. This page explains how Party uses your Strava data when you choose to connect your account.

What we request

When you connect Strava, Party asks for the following scopes: read, activity:read_all, and profile:read_all. This gives the app access to:

  • Your basic athlete profile
  • Your list of bikes (gear)
  • Your activities (rides)

This data is used only to match your Strava rides and gear to the bikes and components you track in Party, so mileage-based wear calculations stay accurate without manual entry.

How your data is stored

Your Strava access and refresh tokens are stored securely in your device's Keychain and are never sent to any server other than Strava's own — Party has no backend of its own. Ride and gear data retrieved from Strava is stored locally on your device only.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect Strava at any time from the app's settings. This removes the stored tokens from your device; it does not delete your already-saved local bike and component data.

More information

This page covers the Strava integration specifically. For the full Party privacy policy, see konstantin-toporovskii.github.io/party-privacy-policy.

Use of Strava data through Party is also subject to Strava's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
